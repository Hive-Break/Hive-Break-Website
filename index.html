What we look at

The value is already there.

Most of it is hiding in plain sight. Here's where we look.

  1. 01 — Monetization

    There's real money in the ad breaks. Most channels leave it there.

    We rebuild ad-break placement, end screens, and playlists so the same views earn more. Usually the first thing we find.

  2. 02 — Distribution

    You made it once. It should earn in more than one place.

    We take what you've already produced across platforms and formats. More reach, no more production.

  3. 03 — Localization

    Most of your potential audience doesn't speak your language.

    We localize your catalogue — dubbing, versioning, the market strategy — and open the rooms you're not in yet.

  4. 04 — Brand & Product

    Your audience would buy from you. Most have nothing to sell.

    CPG, community apps, merchandise — we build the products your audience already wants, and the company to run them.

  5. 05 — Catalog & Capital

    Your catalogue has a price. You should know it.

    We value your library, take it to market, and negotiate the deal — $150M+ closed for creators. If you'd rather raise than sell, we structure that too.
