Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 13, 2026

Hive Break ("Hive Break," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website (hivebreak.com), why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies only to this website and our related communications — not to any third-party site we link to.

Who we are

Hive Break is a growth partner for YouTube creators, based in Los Angeles, California, United States. If you have any question about this policy or your information, contact us at info@hivebreak.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form, we collect the details you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your YouTube channel URL
  • Your organization or company (optional)
  • What you're interested in and the context you share about your goals
  • Any links you choose to include (e.g., a deck or portfolio)
  • The fact that you consented to this policy, with the date and time

We only ask for what we need to evaluate a potential engagement and respond to you thoughtfully. Please don't send us sensitive personal information through the form.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data when you visit — for example, your IP address, browser type, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. This is used to keep the site secure and reliable.

Cookies and fonts

This site does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We load typefaces from Google Fonts to display the site; when we do, your browser contacts Google's servers, which means Google may receive your IP address. You can control or block cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended if you do.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and prepare a personal, relevant reply
  • To evaluate whether we're a good fit to work together
  • To communicate with you about your inquiry and our services
  • To operate, secure, and improve our website
  • To comply with our legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

Legal bases (for visitors in the EEA / UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: your consent (which you give when you submit the form and which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and running our business; and, where relevant, compliance with a legal obligation.

How we share your information

We share your information only with the service providers that help us operate, and only as needed:

  • Brevo — our email and contact-management provider. Your contact-form submission is delivered to us and stored in Brevo so we can manage and respond to your inquiry. Brevo processes this data on our behalf.
  • Hostinger — our website hosting provider, which operates the servers that run this site.
  • Authorities or others — if required by law, to enforce our rights, or to protect the safety of others.
  • Business transfers — if Hive Break is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

Data retention

We keep your inquiry for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our communications, and thereafter only as long as we have a legitimate business or legal reason to do so. You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

International transfers

We are based in the United States, and our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union, or elsewhere. Where data is transferred across borders, we rely on our providers' safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) to protect it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise any of these, email us at info@hivebreak.com and we'll respond as required by applicable law. You can also unsubscribe from any non-essential email we send by using the link in that email.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for businesses and professionals and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to third-party sites (for example, YouTube). We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be made prominent on this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Email us at info@hivebreak.com.
